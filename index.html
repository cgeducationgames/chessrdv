About

ChessRDV is a focused training companion for the opening phase of the game. Pick lines from a curated tree of openings, drill them as White or Black, and let spaced repetition surface the moves you are about to forget — before you forget them at the board.

Features

Opening explorer

Browse a tree of named openings and variations. Pick the lines you want in your repertoire.

Memorizer

Drill your selected lines move by move. Quiet, distraction-free practice.

Spaced repetition

Lines you struggle with come back sooner. Lines you know come back later.

Daily reminders

Optional notification when openings are due — show up, do five minutes, keep your edge.

Side picked for you

Train as the side to move after the last book move — no fiddly color toggles, just the position you’d face over the board.

Offline

Everything runs on-device. No account, no tracking your games.

Who it’s for

Club players who keep blanking on move 8. Improvers who want a serious repertoire without buying a video course. Anyone who has ever closed a chess book, felt confident, and lost the thread two days later.
